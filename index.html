Meanwhile

In 2024 California's Attorney General sued ExxonMobil, alleging the company spent decades selling recycling as the answer to plastic while knowing most plastic would never be recycled. The case is ongoing.

Nationally, about 5% of plastic waste gets recycled. It has never been above 9%.

So ask.

  • Why does it have the arrows on it?
  • Who decided you should rinse it?
  • Where does your city's bin actually go?
  • Why are we still making it?
Plastic is the problem. Paper, cardboard and glass mostly do get recycled, so keep sorting those. Bottles and cans you return for CRV aren't counted here and do better.
